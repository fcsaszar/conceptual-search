* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A mobile app that helps basketball players discover nearby games, check in at courts, and build verified rankings and leaderboards to motivate play and community connection.

## Problem

The creators describe a gap for everyday players---parents, kids, and recreational athletes---who struggle to find reliable, pickup-friendly games and a way to track progress beyond informal word-of-mouth. They frame this as both a logistics problem (knowing where and when people are playing) and a motivation problem (recognition, friendly competition, and belonging).

## Solution / Product Overview

The project proposes a mobile app that maps active courts in real time, enables player check-ins to signal activity, and maintains verified rankings so users can "climb" leaderboards. It aims to turn empty courts into active meetups and to give players a persistent identity anchored in participation and results.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   Real-time court mapping to show where games are happening.

-   Player check-ins to validate court activity and attendance.

-   Verified rankings and leaderboards to track progression.

-   Social elements for sharing moments and celebrating wins (as described).

-   A potential future enhancement: AI-driven stat insights, contingent on later development milestones.

## Target Users & Use Cases

The creators call out recreational players across ages, including parents returning to the game and kids discovering it. Primary scenarios include finding immediate games near home, signaling interest by checking in, and competing on leaderboards with local peers.

## Development Status

According to the page, a working prototype has been tested with roughly 50 local players. A teaser site and an AI demo video are mentioned. The roadmap cites a beta release targeted for November 2025, followed by growth to tens of thousands of downloads and several thousand active users in early 2026.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

Planned milestones include:

-   A phased beta beginning November 2025, emphasizing feedback-driven fixes.

-   Marketing pushes via social channels and in-person pop-up events.

-   Post-beta growth goals into early 2026.

-   A potential later phase unlocking AI stat insights after initial targets are met.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds are intended to: strengthen real-time mapping and ranking capabilities on a no-code platform; run targeted social-media advertising to drive initial downloads; and host regional pop-up events for hands-on testing and family-focused engagement.

## Risks & Challenges

Acknowledged risks include: technical bugs in the no-code stack during beta (mitigated by phased testing and feedback loops); difficulty gaining adoption in a crowded sports-tech space (addressed through targeted ads and local events); potential delays in fulfilling non-digital perks (addressed via on-demand production partners and sequencing digital items first); and founder bandwidth constraints (managed by strict milestone prioritization and openness to adding help).

## Team & Partnerships

The effort is led by the founders as a lean, solo-driven initiative leveraging no-code development. The page references outreach to recognizable sports-media partners and print-on-demand suppliers to amplify awareness and streamline merchandise fulfillment.

## What's Included

Access to the app's core experience: real-time court maps, player check-ins, and verified rankings/leaderboards as described for launch.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The creators emphasize iterative development, with phased testing, user feedback loops, and ongoing updates after launch to improve reliability and expand features.

A mobile app that helps basketball players discover nearby games, check in at courts, and build verified rankings and leaderboards to motivate play and community connection.

## Problem
The creators describe a gap for everyday players---parents, kids, and recreational athletes---who struggle to find reliable, pickup-friendly games and a way to track progress beyond informal word-of-mouth. They frame this as both a logistics problem (knowing where and when people are playing) and a motivation problem (recognition, friendly competition, and belonging).

## Solution / Product Overview
The project proposes a mobile app that maps active courts in real time, enables player check-ins to signal activity, and maintains verified rankings so users can "climb" leaderboards. It aims to turn empty courts into active meetups and to give players a persistent identity anchored in participation and results.

## Key Features & Capabilities
- Real-time court mapping to show where games are happening.
- Player check-ins to validate court activity and attendance.
- Verified rankings and leaderboards to track progression. To ensure integrity, a lightweight verification method is proposed for the beta test, such as requiring a second user present at the game to confirm a result before it updates the leaderboards.
- Social elements for sharing moments and celebrating wins (as described).
- A proposed, simple feature for the beta test: a 'Game Creator' button that lets a user declare an intent to play at a specific court and time.
- A potential future enhancement: AI-driven stat insights, contingent on later development milestones.

## Target Users & Use Cases
The creators call out recreational players across ages, including parents returning to the game and kids discovering it. Primary scenarios include finding immediate games near home, signaling interest by checking in, and competing on leaderboards with local peers.

## Development Status & Beta Testing Hypothesis
According to the page, a working prototype has been tested with roughly 50 local players. A teaser site and an AI demo video are mentioned. The roadmap cites a beta release targeted for November 2025. To validate core engagement and local network density, we plan to test specific hypotheses with the first 100 beta users: we hypothesize that 70% of beta testers will check in at a court at least twice a week. As a proposed metric to demonstrate local network density, we plan to track the average check-in distance from a user's home location, with a target of under 5 miles to demonstrate hyper-local density.

To further test engagement drivers, we propose incorporating a simple gamification element during the beta phase, such as a 'Court Streak' badge awarded for consecutive weekly check-ins. To quantitatively demonstrate its motivational impact, we propose testing a specific target: aiming for a 20% increase in weekly check-ins among users who earn the badge. The proposed lightweight verification method for rankings will also be tested as part of the beta to assess its feasibility and user acceptance.

We also propose to test the new 'Game Creator' feature during the beta. A specific target metric to measure its effectiveness is proposed: tracking how many declared games result in at least one other user checking in.

To directly measure the 'motivation problem,' we propose a specific, small-scale test during the beta: surveying users on their sense of belonging or competitive drive before and after using the app for a month, with a target of a 15% improvement in self-reported motivation scores.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan
Planned milestones include:
- A phased beta beginning November 2025, emphasizing feedback-driven fixes and testing the engagement and network-density hypotheses, including the proposed gamification test (with its 20% increase target), the verification method for rankings, the proposed test of the 'Game Creator' feature and its target metric, and the proposed survey to measure improvement in motivation scores.
- Marketing pushes via social channels and in-person pop-up events.
- Post-beta growth goals into early 2026.
- A potential later phase unlocking AI stat insights after initial targets are met.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds
Funds are intended to: strengthen real-time mapping and ranking capabilities on a no-code platform; run targeted social-media advertising to drive initial downloads; and host regional pop-up events for hands-on testing and family-focused engagement.

## Risks & Challenges
Acknowledged risks include: technical bugs in the no-code stack during beta (mitigated by phased testing and feedback loops); difficulty gaining adoption in a crowded sports-tech space (addressed through targeted ads and local events); potential delays in fulfilling non-digital perks (addressed via on-demand production partners and sequencing digital items first); and founder bandwidth constraints (managed by strict milestone prioritization and openness to adding help). The proposed verification method may introduce friction; this will be monitored during beta testing.

## Team & Partnerships
The effort is led by the founders as a lean, solo-driven initiative leveraging no-code development. The page references outreach to recognizable sports-media partners and print-on-demand suppliers to amplify awareness and streamline merchandise fulfillment.

## What's Included
Access to the app's core experience: real-time court maps, player check-ins, and verified rankings/leaderboards as described for launch.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans
The creators emphasize iterative development, with phased testing, user feedback loops, and ongoing updates after launch to improve reliability and expand features.
