* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary
A platform that provides verifiable proof of ethical sourcing by creating a permanent, independently verifiable record anchored to a public ledger, while building an interactive ecosystem with educational tools and an AI mentor for supply chain professionals and impact-focused organizations.

## Problem
The venture plans to address a digital landscape saturated with manipulation and duplication, where it is increasingly difficult to establish what is original or real. The central challenge is enabling people to credibly prove the authenticity and first possession of a digital record—such as a donation confirmation or an impact report—amid pervasive skepticism and the ease of generating false claims. The venture also plans to address the specific and growing risk of regulatory penalties and legal liability for brands that make unsubstantiated or misleading sustainability claims about their supply chains, which lack a universally trusted, independent verification mechanism.

## Target Users & Use Cases
The venture plans to target both brands seeking to validate their supply chains and consumers looking for trustworthy ethical goods. A planned, specific target user group for initial outreach is procurement teams within consumer goods companies participating in sustainability certification or reporting programs, who need a streamlined way to discover vetted suppliers and provide indisputable proof of their ethical sourcing actions to qualify for certifications or meet regulatory requirements. The venture also plans to explore targeting non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and impact-focused philanthropic foundations as a distinct user group, who require independently verifiable, tamper-proof records of field-level sourcing events and material provenance to substantiate their impact reports to donors, auditors, and grant-making bodies.

## Solution / Product Overview
The product is a platform designed to provide verifiable proof of ethical sourcing. Its core innovation is pairing physical goods with a privacy-preserving digital ledger. When a material is sourced or a production milestone is reached, a fingerprint of the provenance data is created and anchored to a public, time-stamped record, allowing for independent verification without exposing proprietary or sensitive operational details. The venture plans to explore the development of a simple, open-source verification widget that any third-party website (like a brand's product page or a retailer's site) could embed to allow visitors to independently verify the timestamp and authenticity of a claimed sourcing fingerprint directly from that page.

## Delivery Model
The venture plans a lightweight, privacy-first implementation. It would not store sensitive proprietary files or continuous supply-chain data streams. Instead, it would generate compact, anonymized fingerprints of that data for ledger anchoring. The goal is a secure and resource-conscious design that maintains long-term verifiability with minimal infrastructure. The team plans to build a clean public interface and supporting infrastructure to make the verification tools usable by its target audience.

## Revenue Logic
The venture plans to generate revenue through the sale of a subscription service to brands and suppliers for access to the verification ledger, advanced provenance reporting features, and supply chain management capabilities within the platform. An initial free trial period for the subscription service is proposed to onboard supplier and brand partners. The venture also plans to explore potential optional upgrades for enhanced connectivity or integration services for enterprise procurement or certification programs.

## Distinctiveness
The venture aims to differentiate itself by offering heirloom-quality goods that merge immediate, tangible material qualities with enduring digital proof of ethical sourcing. Its proposed system does not just provide a product; it aims to create a permanent, independently verifiable record that the materials were sourced according to specific ethical standards at a specific time, addressing the credibility problem of sustainability claims. This focus on verifiable provenance from a specific, ethical source is presented as a unique safeguard for both consumer trust and material truth.
