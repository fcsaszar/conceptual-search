* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A children's storytelling system that pairs a creativity-first print book with a customizable app offering interactive narration and augmented reality to help kids co-create their own stories while supporting pediatric care and research.

## Problem

The creators argue that children's publishing underutilizes digital interactivity and often fails to "meet kids where they are" in their learning journeys. They highlight the need to blend modern technology with traditional competencies---such as social-emotional learning, emotional regulation, innovation, growth mindset, and problem solving---so children can learn differently and more effectively.

## Solution / Product Overview

The project combines a traditionally published, globally distributable children's book that leaves space for kids to draw their own illustrations with a companion app. The app is positioned as a licensable platform for authors and publishers, expanding print stories into a digital world with interactive storytelling, narration, augmented reality features, and a future library integration. A portion of book sales is committed to supporting a pediatric research hospital.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   Print book designed with blank spaces for child-created illustrations, producing a one-of-a-kind keepsake.

-   App features: interactive storytelling, narration, augmented reality, and planned library integration.

-   Platform vision: app licensable for "infinite stories worldwide."

-   Education-aligned framing: supports social-emotional learning and related competencies.

-   Environmental commitments: request for recycled/sustainably sourced paper, eco-friendly inks/processes; digital formats cited as lowering impact.

-   Donation commitment: 10% of book sales directed to a pediatric research hospital.

## Target Users & Use Cases

Intended for children and the adults who guide their learning (e.g., educators). Use cases include co-creating illustrations in the print book, engaging with narrated and AR-enhanced stories in the app, and leveraging interactive elements to support differentiated learning and creativity.

## Development Status

A prototype of the app exists, and an initial print of a self-published story has been produced. The campaign positions the technology as patent-pending.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

The team states that additional funding is needed to transform the existing prototype into a fully built application and to conduct front- and back-end marketing. The long-term goal is to make the app licensable for all books, enabling broad adoption once the product moves from production stage to shipment.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds are sought to complete application development and to support marketing required for launch and distribution. The project also seeks support for integrating AI capabilities within the app.

## Risks & Challenges

Primary risks include insufficient funding for app development and marketing. The team notes that once the prototype is turned into a "actionable application," growth opportunities expand, but reaching that stage depends on securing resources.

## Team & Partnerships

The venture is described as purpose-driven and female-founded, led by an educator. The app is envisioned to be licensed broadly to authors and publishers; no specific partners are named.

## Technical Details

Stated technologies include narration and augmented reality within the app; the project also references AI-generated content for prototypes and plans to "unlock" AI to fuel children's imagination in the finished application. No device, OS, or compatibility specifications are listed.
