* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

An online learning platform focused on human-centered AI education is evolving into an interactive, creator-driven ecosystem with a personal AI mentor, a hands-on agent playground, and tools for educators to publish micro-courses.

## Problem

The creators argue that AI is reshaping work faster than people can build the skills to keep up. They state that while technology changes daily, human learning lags behind, leaving individuals and teams without practical, up-to-date guidance on using AI effectively.

## Solution / Product Overview

The project expands an existing AI-learning platform into "2.0," positioning it as a social learning ecosystem that blends live AI news, scrollable micro-lessons, certification tracks, and interactive tools. The goal is to make AI learning accessible, simple, and guided by creators and a personal AI mentor.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   **AI Mentor:** A personalized guide that adapts to learner goals, recommends lessons, and supports practice of real-world skills.

-   **AI Agent Playground:** A space to experiment with automations (e.g., email summarization, invoice assistance, social media agents), with 10+ templates, tutorials, and progress tracking.

-   **Course Builder for Creators:** Tools for educators to build, publish, and monetize micro-courses, positioning the platform as creator-driven.

-   **Learning Content & Certifications:** Micro-courses, reels, and structured certifications (e.g., AI foundations and automation tracks) presented with stated durations and skills coverage.

-   **Enterprise Programs:** Mention of programs tailored to teams, including domain-specific learning (e.g., finance).

## Target Users & Use Cases

Individual learners seeking practical AI fluency; creators who want to teach and monetize AI courses; and professional teams pursuing strategic adoption of AI in functions such as marketing and finance.

## Development Status

The team says it has already built a foundation that includes live updates, micro-courses, and certifications. The campaign funds new "2.0" capabilities: AI Mentor and Agent Playground prototypes, expanded creator tools, and a global challenge.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

A four-phase timeline is provided: (1) AI Mentor prototype (February 2026), (2) AI Agent Playground launch (April 2026), (3) Creator Course Builder (June 2026), and (4) Global Challenge plus Certification 2.0 (September 2026).

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds support development of the Mentor and Playground prototypes, expansion of creator tools and certification, a first global fluency challenge, and design of a human-centered interface.

## Risks & Challenges

Identified risks include integrating secure, scalable AI systems, designing intuitive user experiences, and ensuring fairness and accessibility. Mitigations cited are partnering with experienced AI developers and educators, monthly updates, and offering early testing opportunities.

## Team & Partnerships

The project is presented as a global effort with contributors, interns, and partners. It notes collaboration with experienced AI developers and educators, and plans to work with responsible, ethics-minded providers.

## What's Included

Access to the platform's learning environment, including micro-courses, reels, certification tracks, the forthcoming AI Mentor, the Agent Playground with templates and tutorials, and creator tools for course publishing.

## Technical Details

Content is cloud-delivered; the team highlights use of cloud infrastructure powered by renewable energy, emphasis on digital certificates, and reusability of learning progress across career changes.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The team commits to open communication, monthly updates, and opportunities for early testing. It also discloses use of AI in content creation with human review and reliance on licensed or open datasets.
