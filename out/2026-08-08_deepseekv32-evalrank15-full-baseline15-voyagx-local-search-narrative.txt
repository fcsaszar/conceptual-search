* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A social travel app that helps people plan trips together, split costs, discover community-recommended spots, and meet compatible travel companions.

## Problem

The creators argue that trip planning is fragmented and often isolating, especially when coordinating with others, managing shared expenses, and finding authentic places beyond typical booking sites. They frame the gap as the lack of a single place to connect with fellow travelers, organize group logistics, and surface lesser-known destinations from real people.

## Solution / Product Overview

The product is presented as a "new kind of travel app" that combines social profiles, group travel spaces, in-app discovery, and messaging. Its goal is to "turn strangers into travel companions" and make every part of the journey "easier and more social." The campaign emphasizes that the core foundation exists and that additional features are being built to transform group travel.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   **Group travel ("Troupes")**: Dedicated spaces for organizing trips with others.

-   **TripSplit**: In-app shared-expense management for activities, accommodations, and other costs, designed to reduce confusion and simplify payment tracking.

-   **Hidden Gem Locator**: Community-submitted recommendations for unique, lesser-known places (e.g., local cafés, hikes, beaches, neighborhoods); the interface for this feature is still in development.

-   **Discovery, chat, and profiles**: Tools to find travelers, communicate, and showcase interests/experience.

-   **VoyageFund (mentioned)**: Identified as a feature to be finalized, with no further details provided in the campaign.

## Target Users & Use Cases

The app is positioned for solo travelers and groups who want to meet others, organize trips, split expenses, and share travel experiences in one place. Use cases include forming travel groups, coordinating activities, and finding community-endorsed spots at a destination.

## Development Status

The team states that a minimum viable product (core version) has been built and tested internally. Foundational elements---profiles, group travel spaces, and discovery---are in place. The Hidden Gem Locator's user interface remains under development.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

Support from the campaign is intended to complete development, enhance performance, and prepare for a stated global launch in Fall 2026. Listed priorities include finalizing TripSplit, VoyageFund, and the Hidden Gem Locator; improving scalability; refining UI/UX; and conducting marketing, community growth, and early feature testing. The team follows a milestone-driven roadmap with defined delivery targets.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds will be applied to finishing the above features, optimizing cloud infrastructure and performance, refining the interface, and supporting marketing, community expansion, and early testing efforts.

## Risks & Challenges

Stated risks include timeline adjustments due to testing and app-store approvals, dependencies on third-party integrations (such as payment APIs), and the need to scale systems as usage grows. Mitigations cited: a contracted full-stack development team, hosting on a major cloud provider, a milestone-based plan, and budget allocations for testing, QA, and performance optimization.

## Team & Partnerships

The project is led by the founders with a dedicated full-stack development partner under ongoing contract; no individual names or external brands are emphasized beyond that description.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The team commits to open communication and regular updates to keep backers informed as the product progresses toward launch.
