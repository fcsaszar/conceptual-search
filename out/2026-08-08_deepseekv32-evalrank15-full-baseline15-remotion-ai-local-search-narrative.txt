* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A mobile app that uses AI-powered video analysis to deliver sprint coaching feedback anytime, helping athletes improve form while enabling coaches and parents to track progress.

## Problem

The creators describe a consistent gap in sprint training: athletes often practice without timely feedback, while coaches lack bandwidth to analyze every block start or sprint repetition---especially when juggling other jobs or when athletes are traveling or on school breaks. This leaves athletes without guidance to progress safely and effectively when direct coaching isn't available.

## Solution / Product Overview

The product is presented as "a sprint coach in your pocket." It analyzes user-submitted sprint videos with AI to provide actionable feedback at any time. The aim is to let athletes see what's happening in their sprints, help coaches extend their impact without slowing practice, and assure parents that training is progressing safely.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   AI-powered video analysis of sprints.

-   Feedback on block starts.

-   Analysis of shin and torso angles.

-   Insights into max-velocity mechanics.

-   Positioning as a tool to complement---not replace---human coaching.

## Target Users & Use Cases

The campaign calls out athletes who train alone or while traveling, coaches seeking scalable analysis during practice, and parents looking for confidence in safe, continuous improvement.

## Development Status

The team states it has a working demo. Funds are intended to "complete and polish" the app into a user-ready tool, with a launch planned for the first wave of athletes, coaches, and parents.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

Planned milestones include: finalizing the app, building specified analysis features (block starts, angle analysis, max-velocity mechanics), partnering with athletes/coaches/influencers to spread the word, and launching to an initial user group. The estimated delivery is December 2025, with a contingency that delivery could shift slightly into January 2026. If timelines shift, the team commits to immediate notifications and monthly progress updates during development.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Most funds are allocated to app development, with additional support for launch preparation and marketing. The team notes a buffer for unexpected costs to keep development the top priority.

## Risks & Challenges

The team cites typical software development risks such as potential delays or technical issues, mitigated by a scoped plan and monthly updates. Because rewards are mostly digital (codes or access), fulfillment risk is described as low, though minor delays in distributing access could occur. A health and wellness disclaimer clarifies that the project is not a substitute for medical treatment or diagnosis.

## Team & Partnerships

The effort is led by a sprint coach ("the founders"). The team plans to collaborate with athletes, coaches, and influencers to expand reach.

## What's Included

Backers receive primarily digital rewards, such as access codes.

## Technical Details

The campaign labels the technology as patent pending and repeatedly emphasizes AI-based video analysis for sprint mechanics. No device, OS, or hardware specs are provided.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The team promises ongoing updates during development and indicates that improvements will be delivered through the app over time.
