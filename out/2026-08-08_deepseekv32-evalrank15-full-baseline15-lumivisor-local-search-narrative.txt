* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A flexible, transparent LED film and controller that mounts to a helmet visor to display preset expressions while remaining see-through for the wearer during daytime use.

## Problem

The creators say riders and performers lack a way to express emotions or communicate visually from behind a visor without sacrificing visibility or comfort; prior attempts were bulky, opaque, or impractical.

## Solution / Product Overview

The product is a transparent, flexible LED film paired with a compact controller and battery pack that attaches to the inside of a visor via suction cups. It shows animated faces and patterns to people outside the helmet while allowing the user to see through during daylight hours.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   Transparent LED array on a clear flexible circuit; designed so the wearer can see through it.

-   17 selectable patterns/expressions, with a default neutral "eyes" animation that resumes automatically after about 10 seconds.

-   Daytime-only operation (dawn to dusk); creators emphasize high daytime visibility and "brilliantly bright" output.

-   Four suction cups provide a non-permanent, residue-free install that can be removed and reattached quickly.

-   Transferable between helmets; additional mounting clips for the battery pack are available as an add-on.

-   Inside view characterized by well-spaced LEDs; creators note a slight halo when lit but say interference is minimal.

-   Explanation of perceptual "tuning out" (Troxler effect) to describe how fixed LED elements fade from the wearer's awareness when focusing on the road ahead.

## Target Users & Use Cases

Positioned for riders, cosplayers, performers, and exhibitors seeking expressive visuals on a visor in controlled environments, off-road settings, or private property---not for public-road operation.

## Development Status

The founders describe a six-year development path from rigid boards to flexible film, culminating in a "production-ready" design. Multiple prototype units have been produced and tested; the current controller is described as "sleek" with straightforward on/off and pattern selection.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

The team states production will commence once funding is secured and believes the path to market will be "fairly seamless," based on prior prototype production runs. No detailed schedule beyond this is provided.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds are sought to move from prototypes to production manufacturing of the film and associated electronics and mounts.

## Risks & Challenges

The creators characterize risks as minimal, citing lined-up production and experience building prototype units. They nonetheless include extensive safety and legality disclaimers and stress the product is not certified for public-road use, not for nighttime, and should not be used in ways that impair vision or distract. Users assume responsibility for compliance with applicable laws.

## Team & Partnerships

The product is led by the founders, who recount sourcing and iterating with a factory capable of placing LEDs on flexible circuits. No named partners are listed in the campaign narrative.

## What's Included

Materials shown and referenced include: the flexible LED visor film, a controller, a battery pack and receiver (shown in an unboxing), four suction cups for mounting, and optional extra battery-pack mounting clips as add-ons.

## Technical Details

-   Transparent flexible PCB with surface-mounted LEDs and copper traces.

-   Non-adhesive installation via four suction cups.

-   Daytime-only operational guidance; not for nighttime or low-visibility environments.

-   Behavior: pattern auto-reverts to default after a short duration.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The team invites backer feedback to shape future development but provides no specific post-launch support or update cadence beyond general campaign communications.
