* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A subscription-based hive-sponsorship program and companion app that connects supporters with local beekeepers to strengthen pollination, aid farmers, and deliver seasonal honey from a sponsored hive.

## Problem

The campaign states that bees pollinate a significant share of the human food supply, yet populations are declining. It cites climate stressors and other factors leading to annual colony losses for urban beekeepers, along with high winter mortality linked to pesticide exposure and limited forage. The creators also argue that most store-bought honey is processed and blended, obscuring the unique character of local varieties, and that people have grown disconnected from food sources and the role of pollinators in agriculture.

## Solution / Product Overview

The project proposes a simple path to participation: sponsor a hive through the app and receive honey harvested from that hive. The company places and maintains hives via professional beekeepers, provides periodic updates, and channels support toward pollination services that benefit local biodiversity and crop resilience while offering tangible rewards. No beekeeping experience is required.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   Sponsor packages for individuals, families, schools, and businesses.

-   Professional placement and maintenance of hives in suitable habitats such as farms, gardens, and meadows.

-   Regular updates about the hive, including photos, videos, and reports on health and production.

-   Honey yields tied to sponsorship level, ranging from seasonal honey to curated gift boxes.

-   Educational materials and opportunities to visit a hive under expert guidance.

-   App roadmap features including an activity-tracking dashboard with visual data and a feedback system.

## Target Users & Use Cases

Eco-conscious individuals, families, schools, companies, and local farmers seeking direct support for pollination, educational engagement, or corporate sustainability initiatives are explicitly referenced.

## Development Status

The team plans to develop a minimum viable technology platform and initial branding while assembling beekeeper partnerships; the page does not claim a finished product.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

-   Phase 1 (0--6 months): establish beekeeper/apiary partnerships in one state, build an MVP platform, and create initial marketing.

-   Phase 2 (6--12 months): enhance the platform with an activity dashboard, add a feedback system, and expand the beekeeper network.

-   Phase 3 (12--18 months): expand to a multi-state region, introduce premium offerings, and launch a corporate program. Additionally, the team aims to deploy hundreds of hives across the region by 2026.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds will support hive placement and maintenance, technology development, beekeeper partnerships, creation of new bee habitats, research on bee health, education in schools and communities, and sustainable beekeeping practices.

## Risks & Challenges

The campaign lists regulatory/permit timing, high winter mortality (about 40%), pesticide exposure, limited forage, and climate-driven bloom variability causing 20--50% yield fluctuations as key risks.

## Team & Partnerships

The project is led by the founders and implemented with professional beekeepers, with plans to formalize partnerships with several beekeepers and apiaries.

## What's Included

Depending on sponsorship level: seasonal honey, exclusive gift boxes, educational materials, regular hive updates, and guided visit opportunities.

## Technical Details

Planned environmental commitments include hive construction from natural (non-plastic) materials with reuse/refurbishment of existing hives; biodegradable or compostable packaging (e.g., sugarcane pulp); and prioritization of local deliveries.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

Ongoing communications include regular hive updates and, as the platform evolves, an activity dashboard and feedback mechanisms; the team also plans educational programming and a corporate offering.
