* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A compact, portable home-security device that installs without tools or Wi-Fi, offering app control, on-device alerts, and shared access for renters, students, and travelers.

## Problem

The creators argue that traditional residential security is expensive, invasive, and built for homeowners, leaving students, renters, and travelers without a practical way to feel secure in dorms, sublets, and temporary stays. They cite barriers such as landlord approval, drilling, and long-term contracts as key blockers.

## Solution / Product Overview

The product is a lease-friendly smart security device designed to be set up in minutes without tools or approvals. It works without Wi-Fi (with an optional cellular version) and provides peace of mind across dorms, apartments, and travel accommodations through mobile alerts, local alarms, and simple controls.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   **Burglar detection:** When a door opens, the system checks for nearby approved devices (e.g., a user's phone); unrecognized entries are flagged as unauthorized.

-   **Versatile keypad:** Users can arm/disarm without a phone.

-   **Lock reminder:** Sends a notification if the user leaves a defined safe zone without locking the door.

-   **Alarm system:** Triggers an on-device alarm upon intrusion and sends instant mobile alerts to approved users.

-   **Real-time tracking:** Shows last-seen time/place, enables a "ping" to locate the device, and includes a Lost Mode.

-   **Multi-user/device access:** Multiple approved users can control the device and receive notifications.

-   **Cellular connectivity (optional):** Maintains alerts during internet outages; paired with the built-in battery for continued operation.

-   **Mobile app control:** Monitor status, receive alerts, and adjust settings remotely.

## Target Users & Use Cases

Stated audiences include college students, renters, and travelers using dorms, apartments, sublets, and short-term stays. The device is positioned for everyday protection in shared or temporary spaces and for situations with unreliable networks.

## Development Status

According to the page, development began in late 2022. The team references "final prototypes," a brief demo, and a product evolution overview.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

The team states it is moving from final prototypes into scaled production and has prepared contingency plans for potential schedule shifts, supported by relationships with suppliers and manufacturing partners. No specific calendar dates are listed.

## Risks & Challenges

Acknowledged risks include supply-chain delays during scale-up and the complexity of supporting multi-user, multi-device scenarios across varied environments. Mitigations include established partner relationships and a focus on keeping the experience simple but secure.

## Team & Partnerships

The founders describe themselves as students and renters who built the product around their lived constraints. They reference suppliers and manufacturing partners without naming them. The team also discloses the use of an AI text-to-speech system for a campaign video voice-over.

## What's Included

A mobile app experience for remote control/alerts is described. The campaign also mentions an initial free trial period for the product's subscription service. Variants include a Wi-Fi model and an option with cellular connectivity.

## Technical Details

Explicit details include optional cellular connectivity, an on-device keypad, an on-device alarm, approved-device proximity checks, and a built-in battery supporting operation during internet outages. Specific dimensions, materials, and OS requirements are not listed.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The team emphasizes ongoing commitment to a simple, secure experience as the product scales; ongoing software update cadence or warranty terms are not detailed.

## Key Features & Capabilities
-   **Burglar detection:** When a door opens, the system checks for nearby approved devices (e.g., a user's phone); unrecognized entries are flagged as unauthorized.
-   **Versatile keypad:** Users can arm/disarm without a phone.
-   **Lock reminder:** Sends a notification if the user leaves a defined safe zone without locking the door.
-   **Alarm system:** Triggers an on-device alarm upon intrusion and sends instant mobile alerts to approved users.
-   **Real-time tracking:** Shows last-seen time/place, enables a "ping" to locate the device, and includes a Lost Mode.
-   **Multi-user/device access:** Multiple approved users can control the device and receive notifications.
-   **Cellular connectivity (optional):** Maintains alerts during internet outages; paired with the built-in battery for continued operation.
-   **Mobile app control:** Monitor status, receive alerts, and adjust settings remotely.
