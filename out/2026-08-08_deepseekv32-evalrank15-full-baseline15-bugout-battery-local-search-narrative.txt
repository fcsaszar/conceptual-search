* Step 0: Initial state

## One-Sentence Summary

A rugged, portable, off-grid battery-and-solar power system promises dependable electricity for emergencies, RVs, tiny homes, and other mobile or remote setups, emphasizing durability over consumer gadgetry.

## Problem

The creators argue that many portable power solutions are fragile, plastic, and not built for real-world stress, leaving people vulnerable during disasters or when living or traveling off the grid. They position reliable backup power as essential for "freedom seekers," homesteaders, road-trippers, and anyone preparing for uncertainty.

## Solution / Product Overview

The product is an all-in-one, off-grid solar power system encased in industrial-grade diamond plate. It's presented as "not a toy," designed to deliver industrial-strength, mobile electricity for appliances, tools, and devices, whether in an RV, a tiny home, or emergency scenarios. Assembly is stated to occur in the U.S.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   High power output rated at 3.8 kW for running appliances, tools, and devices.

-   Off-grid readiness with up to 1,800 W solar input, enabling operation without grid access.

-   Rugged construction using industrial components within a diamond-plate enclosure.

-   Mobile design marketed for RVs, vans, and "bug-out" configurations.

-   Large 10-inch solid wheels that never go flat and can traverse rough terrain.

## Target Users & Use Cases

The campaign speaks to RV travelers, tiny-home residents, homesteaders, and preparedness-minded users, as well as anyone needing dependable emergency backup. It also references those who "live beyond the grid" and want power on the go.

## Development Status

The team states the product is in a late beta stage, with several units already in the field. The creators are working part-time on design, assembly, and testing and indicate the enclosure will be further refined and the component supply chain solidified.

## Roadmap & Delivery Plan

Post-funding, the plan is to refine the enclosure design, secure component sourcing, and assemble 100 units within six months following the fundraising period. No additional timelines or certification steps are specified on the page.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Funds are intended to finalize the enclosure, stabilize the supply chain, and scale production capacity to build the planned initial batch of 100 units after the campaign concludes.

## Risks & Challenges

The primary risk identified is securing components. Mitigation includes dual-sourcing domestically where possible and internationally to maintain quality standards and reduce the chance of delays or blockages.

## Team & Partnerships

The effort is led by a small group described as artisans and engineers who design, assemble, and test the product. Specific partner names are not listed, though the text mentions sourcing from both domestic and overseas suppliers.

## Technical Details

-   Output power: 3.8 kW (claimed).

-   Solar capability: up to 1,800 W (claimed).

-   Construction: industrial-grade diamond-plate enclosure with industrial components.

-   Mobility: 10-inch solid, no-flat wheels for rough terrain.

-   Assembly location: U.S. (as stated).
