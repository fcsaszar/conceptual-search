* Step 0: Initial state

--- BEGIN PROJECT ---
## One-Sentence Summary

A mobile app that aggregates local yard, estate, and community sales on a single live map, plans efficient routes, and adds game-like rewards to turn bargain hunting into a community-strengthening adventure.

## Problem

The creators describe weekend "treasure hunts" devolving into frustrating scavenger work: listings are scattered across social groups, random sites, fading street signs, and half-remembered addresses, causing wasted time, fuel, and missed finds. There's no comprehensive, real-time map for all types of sales, making planning difficult and inefficient.

## Solution / Product Overview

The product centralizes every kind of local sale---yard, estate, flea, vendor markets, church bazaars, and pop-ups---onto one interactive map. It helps users discover events any day of the week, plot an organized route, and enjoy a "modern treasure hunt" that also channels foot traffic to nearby small businesses.

## Key Features & Capabilities

-   Unified discovery of multiple sale types (yard, estate, flea, vendor fairs, church bazaars, neighborhood pop-ups).
-   Route planning to streamline multi-stop outings.
-   Gamification: surprise "Treasure Drops," collectible "doubloons," community leaderboards, and an in-app redemption area ("Plunder Pantry") for real-world merchandise.
-   Local-business support: optional geo-fenced ads that surface modest, location-triggered offers from nearby shops.
-   Emphasis on reuse/upcycling by keeping quality goods in circulation and out of landfills.

## Target Users & Use Cases

Designed for families, casual shoppers, and serious resellers seeking efficient discovery of local sales; for community explorers who want an engaging weekend activity; and for nearby small businesses aiming to reach shoppers traveling between sale stops. A planned expansion of the target user base includes **municipal waste management and sustainability departments** as potential users, who could utilize aggregated, anonymized data on reuse activity to measure community diversion from landfills and inform local circular economy initiatives.

## Why Crowdfunding / Use of Funds

Support is framed as enabling development of a single-map hub that helps shoppers, sellers, and local businesses thrive together; energizes neighborhood commerce; and advances reuse. The team also cites efforts to build an audience via community connections and targeted marketing.

## Risks & Challenges

-   **App development & dependencies:** Feature additions, testing needs, or changes by third-party mapping/payment services could cause delays. Mitigation: use an experienced development team, set clear milestones, and provide regular updates.
-   **Physical reward fulfillment:** Merchandise depends on printing/production/shipping schedules. Mitigation: reliable suppliers already sourced; timelines to be confirmed before fulfillment; prompt communication and timeline adjustments if needed.
-   **Audience growth:** Adoption depends on attracting both shoppers and sellers. Mitigation: active social outreach, local-business partnerships, and planned marketing campaigns. The creators commit to transparent communication, regular progress reports, and keeping supporters informed throughout.

## Team & Partnerships

The project is led by the founders, drawing on an experienced development team for build/testing. The page mentions relationships with reliable merchandise suppliers and outreach to local businesses; no specific organizations or individuals are named.

## Support & Post-Launch Plans

The team promises regular updates and open communication about progress and any changes to delivery expectations.
